    How to play

    Capture the enemy Nexus. Choose a species privately. Blue goes first. Each side places three Proxies in its green Cradle Sector.

    Background

    Humanity depends on AI when it discovers hostile powers beyond Earth. With little intelligence and no agreement on how to respond, the world's governments turn their supercomputers over to their most advanced AI. It simulates possible wars, including conflicts between the alien powers. Each match is one of those simulations.

    TV stands for teravolts. Recharge 3 TV plus 1 TV per active Proxy, up to 40 TV or 20 TV with The Call of Void. Each side has three minutes to act. The turn number advances after both sides finish.

    Move · 1 TV
    Enter an adjacent hex. Landing on an enemy Proxy removes it immediately and earns one reserve Proxy. Enemy shields and your own Proxies block movement.
    Deploy · base cost 1 TV
    Place a reserve Proxy on any empty Cradle hex, including Obelisks. It activates next turn. The Call of Void makes all deployments free.
    Nexus
    Enter the enemy Nexus to win.
    Vanguard
    Pay 2 TV to arm one half of the Nexus shield. Fully protect the Nexus by arming and staffing both Vanguards. Each half needs an online home Aegis or Obelisk clear of enemies.
    Sentinel Obelisk
    Capture for 3 TV (4 for Veyari). A Proxy on a controlled home Obelisk can fire up to two hexes away for 1 TV (2 for Directorate).
    Aegis
    Capture for 0 TV. A powered Aegis you control can deploy Proxies with the Forward Deployment augment.
    Deposit
    Occupy for 3 TV to add one Proxy to your reserve.

    Occupied special hexes show an inlay around the Proxy. Look for a crown on a Nexus, side brackets on a Vanguard, pointed chevrons on an Obelisk, flat bars on an Aegis, a gold ring on a Deposit, or a teal diamond on a cache. Select any hex to see its rules and occupants.

    Fabricate a reserve Proxy for 2 TV with fewer than two active Proxies, once every three turns. Salvage caches surface in Turbulence every third turn. An active Proxy can recover one for 2 TV.

    All shields start offline. Select an active Proxy on a generator you control and pay 2 TV to activate its shield. Aegis and Obelisk shields stay active when your Proxy leaves. Capture resets and reverses the arc. The first powered shield on a shared edge keeps it while active.

    Solid borders are powered. Dashed borders show offline shields. Gold segments around a Nexus mark its shielded approaches. A small gold hexagon around a Proxy marks Keth armor, which absorbs one Obelisk or Defensive Strike hit.

    Buy Forward Deployment for 5 TV. An active Proxy on a powered Aegis can deploy to any legal empty neighbor for 1 TV and one reserve. It can deploy repeatedly from the same Aegis.

    Capturing a special hex linked to your territory claims its one-hex ring and connects it to your nearest existing region. Enclosed pockets with no special node join your region, including pockets closed by the board edge. Inspect a node to see its links. Keep a Proxy on a claimed Deposit until your next turn to secure it.

    Species abilities are explained when you choose. Board effects are visible, and the match review reveals both picks.

    Hold a Deposit with an active Proxy to buy augments. Use a purchased augment on one of your turns, even if you later lose the Deposit.

    Buying Hop unlocks it for the match. Select an active Proxy and look for violet diamonds. Pick one or more landings, then confirm the entire chain for 1 TV. Each enemy Proxy you jump over is captured.

    Use Q, W, E, A, S, and D to move around the board with a keyboard. Enter selects a hex.
