    How to play

    Capture the enemy Nexus. Choose a species privately. Blue goes first. Each side places three Proxies in its green Cradle Sector.

    Background

    Humanity depends on AI when it discovers hostile powers beyond Earth. With little intelligence and no agreement on how to respond, the world's governments turn their supercomputers over to their most advanced AI. It simulates possible wars, including conflicts between the alien powers. Each match is one of those simulations.

    TV stands for teravolts. TV is the game's energy budget for orders. Base recharge is 3 TV plus 1 TV per active Proxy, up to 30 TV. Spend TV before the three-minute turn ends. Unused TV is lost.

    Move · 1 TV
    Enter an adjacent hex. Enemy shield borders block movement.
    Strike · base cost 2 TV
    Move onto an enemy hex with enough TV left to fight, then choose Strike to attack the enemy sharing that hex. You can withdraw for free. Ending the turn or running out of time sends your Proxy back to its starting hex.
    Deploy · base cost 1 TV
    Place a reserve Proxy on a free hex in your Cradle. It can act, power nodes, and generate TV starting on your next turn.
    Nexus
    Enter the enemy Nexus to win.
    Vanguard
    A friendly Proxy powers the Nexus shield from here. It falls if the enemy controls all eight of your Obelisks.
    Sentinel Obelisk
    Occupy for 3 TV to shut down enemy shields or restore your own. Fire from your own staffed Obelisk for 1 TV to hit an enemy up to two steps away.
    Aegis
    Enter with an active Proxy to claim it and disable its enemy shield. Friendly control restores the shield.
    Deposit
    Occupy for 3 TV to add one Proxy to your reserve.

    Fabricate a reserve Proxy for 2 TV with fewer than two active Proxies, once every three turns. Salvage caches surface in Turbulence every third round. An active Proxy can recover one for 2 TV.

    A staffed Aegis can deploy a reserve Proxy to an open neighboring hex for 1 TV. Its shields drop until your next turn. Keep a Proxy on a newly claimed node until your next turn to secure control.

    Species abilities are explained when you choose. Board effects are visible, and the match review reveals both picks.

    Hold a Deposit with an active Proxy to buy augments. Use a purchased augment on one of your turns, even if you later lose the Deposit.

    Use Q, W, E, A, S, and D to move around the board with a keyboard. Enter selects a hex.
